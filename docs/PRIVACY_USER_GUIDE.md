# Privacy Features - User Guide

## Quick Start: Understanding Privacy in the School Counselor Ledger

The School Counselor Ledger is designed to protect the privacy of your counseling interactions while still allowing you to collaborate effectively with colleagues. This guide explains what you need to know about privacy features.

## 🔒 What's Private vs. 👥 What's Shared

### Private to You (🔒)
- **Your interaction records** - Notes, observations, session details
- **Your follow-up information** - Personal reminders and plans
- **Your dashboard statistics** - Your personal workload and metrics
- **Your calendar appointments** - Your scheduled interactions

### Shared with Colleagues (👥)
- **Student profiles** - Names, grades, contact information
- **Contact information** - Parents, teachers, administrators
- **Reason categories** - Standardized interaction types
- **School settings** - System configuration and categories

### Admin Access (👨‍💼)
- **Aggregated statistics** - School-wide data without individual details
- **System management** - User accounts and configuration
- **Audit information** - Access logs without private content

## Visual Privacy Indicators

### In the Interface

Look for these indicators to understand data privacy:

- 🔒 **Lock icon** - Your private interactions
- 👥 **People icon** - Shared student/contact data
- 📊 **Chart icon** - Your personal statistics
- 🏫 **Building icon** - School-wide admin data

### Color Coding

- **Blue borders** - Your private data
- **Green backgrounds** - Shared collaborative data
- **Gray text** - System information
- **Red alerts** - Access restrictions or errors

## How Privacy Works for Counselors

### When You View Students

**What You See:**
- ✅ Student's basic information (name, grade, contact details)
- ✅ Your interaction history with the student
- ✅ Your follow-ups for this student
- ✅ Your statistics for this student

**What You Don't See:**
- ❌ Other counselors' interactions with the student
- ❌ Other counselors' private notes
- ❌ Other counselors' follow-up plans

**Example:**
When you view "Sarah Johnson's" profile:
- You see her name, grade 10, parent contact info (shared)
- You see the 3 interactions you've had with her (private)
- You don't see the 2 interactions Ms. Smith had with her (private to Ms. Smith)

### When You View Your Dashboard

**Your Dashboard Shows:**
- Total interactions YOU created
- Students YOU have worked with
- Time YOU have spent
- YOUR category breakdown
- YOUR recent activity

**It Does NOT Show:**
- Other counselors' statistics
- School-wide totals (unless you're an admin)
- Other counselors' activities

### When You Generate Reports

**Your Reports Include:**
- Only interactions you created
- Only students you've worked with
- Only your time and activities
- Your personal trends and patterns

**To Get School-Wide Data:**
- You need administrator access
- Or ask your admin to generate school reports

## How Privacy Works for Administrators

### What Admins Can See

**Aggregated Data:**
- Total interactions across all counselors
- School-wide statistics and trends
- User activity levels (without private details)
- System usage and performance metrics

**Individual Management:**
- User account information
- Role assignments and permissions
- System configuration settings
- Audit logs and security events

### What Admins Cannot See (In Normal Reports)

**Private Counselor Data:**
- Individual counselor's private notes
- Specific interaction content
- Personal counselor observations
- Detailed session information

**Exception:** Admins can access individual interactions if needed for system administration, but this is logged as a security event.

## Common Privacy Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Collaborating on Student Support

**Situation:** You and another counselor both work with the same student.

**How It Works:**
1. You both see the student's profile and contact information
2. You each see only your own interactions with the student
3. You can coordinate through shared student notes (if available)
4. You maintain separate, private interaction records

**Best Practice:** Use shared contact information to coordinate, but keep interaction details private.

### Scenario 2: Administrative Oversight

**Situation:** Your principal wants to see counseling activity data.

**How It Works:**
1. Admin can generate school-wide reports
2. Reports show aggregated statistics without private details
3. Individual counselor performance can be measured
4. Private interaction content remains confidential

**Best Practice:** Admins should use aggregated reports for oversight while respecting counselor privacy.

### Scenario 3: Staff Transitions

**Situation:** A counselor leaves and you need to take over their students.

**How It Works:**
1. You gain access to shared student information immediately
2. Previous counselor's interactions remain private (archived)
3. You start fresh with your own interaction records
4. Admin can provide transition summaries if needed

**Best Practice:** Focus on current student needs rather than historical private records.

## Privacy Settings and Controls

### User Account Settings

**What You Can Control:**
- Your profile information
- Notification preferences
- Display settings
- Password and security

**What You Cannot Control:**
- Privacy policy enforcement (automatic)
- Access to other counselors' data (restricted)
- System-wide settings (admin only)

### Data Export and Backup

**Your Data:**
- You can export your own interaction data
- Reports include only your records
- Backup includes your private information only

**Shared Data:**
- Student/contact exports available to all counselors
- System backups handled by administrators
- Shared data exports don't include private interactions

## Security Best Practices

### Protecting Your Privacy

1. **Strong Passwords**
   - Use unique, complex passwords
   - Don't share your login credentials
   - Change passwords regularly

2. **Secure Sessions**
   - Always log out when finished
   - Don't leave the application open on shared computers
   - Use private/incognito browsing on public computers

3. **Physical Security**
   - Lock your screen when away
   - Don't let others use your logged-in session
   - Be aware of who can see your screen

### Reporting Privacy Concerns

**If You Suspect a Privacy Violation:**
1. Document what you observed
2. Note the date, time, and circumstances
3. Report to your administrator immediately
4. Don't attempt to investigate yourself

**If You Accidentally Access Wrong Data:**
1. Close the information immediately
2. Don't read or copy the information
3. Report the incident to your administrator
4. This helps improve system security

## Understanding Error Messages

### Common Privacy Error Messages

**"Access Denied: You can only view your own interactions"**
- **Meaning:** You tried to access another counselor's private data
- **Action:** Use the back button and access only your own records

**"Student information is shared, but interaction history is private"**
- **Meaning:** You can see the student profile but not other counselors' interactions
- **Action:** This is normal - create your own interactions with the student

**"Insufficient permissions for this report"**
- **Meaning:** You need admin access for school-wide reports
- **Action:** Ask your administrator to generate the report or grant appropriate access

**"Session expired for security"**
- **Meaning:** You were automatically logged out for security
- **Action:** Log back in to continue working

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Privacy Questions

**Q: Can other counselors see my notes?**
A: No, your interaction notes are completely private to you.

**Q: Can I see how many interactions other counselors have?**
A: Only if you're an administrator. Counselors see only their own statistics.

**Q: What happens if I accidentally try to access someone else's data?**
A: The system will deny access and may log the attempt. This is normal security behavior.

**Q: Can I share my interaction data with a colleague?**
A: You can export your own data, but sharing should follow your school's policies.

### Technical Questions

**Q: How do I know if data is private or shared?**
A: Look for privacy indicators (🔒 for private, 👥 for shared) throughout the interface.

**Q: Why can't I see all interactions for a student?**
A: You only see your own interactions. Other counselors' interactions are private to them.

**Q: Can I change privacy settings?**
A: Privacy enforcement is automatic and cannot be disabled. This protects everyone's data.

**Q: What if I need to see another counselor's data for an emergency?**
A: Contact your administrator who can provide appropriate access if necessary.

### Administrative Questions

**Q: How do admins see school-wide data without violating privacy?**
A: Admin reports aggregate data and anonymize individual counselor details.

**Q: Can admins read my private notes?**
A: Admins can access individual records if needed for system administration, but this is logged.

**Q: Who can see the audit logs?**
A: Only administrators can see audit logs, and they don't contain private interaction content.

**Q: How long is data kept?**
A: Contact your administrator for your school's data retention policy.
